A COMBAT-FIRST APPROACH

Nothing should happen
without a reason.

Ashen Crown is built around readable encounters, committed attacks and defensive choices with consequences. The goal is not spectacle for its own sake — every hit, stagger and recovery window exists to make the player pay attention.

03 / COMBAT

Learn the rhythm.

Four layers of interaction create a combat loop that rewards patience without making every encounter slow.

01

Committed strikes

Light attacks flow through a four-hit combo. Heavy attacks charge into a deliberate release window instead of becoming a button mash.

02

Defend with intent

Block, parry or spend stamina to roll. A narrow perfect-dodge window turns timing into a real resource.

03

Break the poise

Damage is more than a health trade. Pressure an enemy's poise, create a stagger and earn your opening.

04

Bosses that change the rules

Three-phase boss logic, enrage states, ranged threats and ground hazards shift the player's priorities rather than simply inflate health.

04 / UNDER THE HOOD

Small systems.
Clear contracts.

The codebase keeps combat modules separated through shared interfaces, making the prototype easier to extend without turning every mechanic into a dependency.
